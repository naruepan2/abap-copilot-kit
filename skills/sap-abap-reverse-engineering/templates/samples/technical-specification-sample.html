2.Technical Overview

The report shows net sales, cost of sales and gross margin for open sales orders. Users filter by company and delivery-date range; the view aggregates amounts per order item.

The mechanism is a four-layer CDS stack reading standard sales tables and enriching customer name and cost from condition records (ZC_DEMO_OPEN_ORDER_MARGIN → ZI_DEMO_OPEN_ORDER_MARGIN → ZI_DEMO_OPEN_ORDER_BASE → physical tables).
